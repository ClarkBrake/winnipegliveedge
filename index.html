About Brake Live Edge

Built by hand. Made to be lived with.

Brake Live Edge creates handcrafted coffee and dining tables that celebrate the natural shape and character of solid wood.

Every slab is individually selected, shaped, sanded, and finished with close attention to detail. Natural features are preserved rather than hidden, giving each piece its own identity.

Whether placed in a living room or gathered around for family meals, each table is built to become part of the home for years to come.
